Hi there! I am Yihan Wang, an M.S. student in Interdisciplinary Data Science (MIDS) at Duke University. I received my B.S. in Applied Mathematics & Computational Sciences (Mathematics Track) from Duke Kunshan University & Duke University in May 2026, where I was advised by Prof. Shixin Xu and Prof. Dongmian Zou.

你好！我是王一涵，现就读于 杜克大学交叉数据科学硕士项目（MIDS）。 我于2026年5月从 昆山杜克大学与 杜克大学 获得应用数学与计算科学（数学方向）理学学士学位， 师从 徐士鑫教授与 邹东勉教授。

I am broadly interested in quantitative finance, NLP-driven financial analytics, and applied machine learning. My work ranges from LLM-based sentiment analysis and data-driven investment strategies to graph and generative methods for scientific data. Feel free to reach out if you have shared research interests!

我的研究兴趣涵盖量化金融、NLP驱动的金融分析与应用机器学习， 既包括基于大语言模型的情感分析与数据驱动的投资策略， 也包括面向科学数据的图方法与生成模型。 如果您有相关的研究兴趣，欢迎联系！
