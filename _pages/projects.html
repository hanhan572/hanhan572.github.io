FinBERT-Fed: Sentiment and Macro Factors around Federal Reserve Events

FinBERT-Fed：美联储事件窗口下的情感与宏观因子

Signature Work, advised by Prof. Shixin Xu, Duke Kunshan University

毕业课题（Signature Work），指导教师：徐士鑫教授，昆山杜克大学

Combined FinBERT sentiment scores with macroeconomic factors to predict cross-sectional stock returns around Federal Reserve announcements.

将 FinBERT 情感得分与宏观经济因子相结合，预测美联储公告前后的股票横截面收益。

Temperature-Induced Phase Transitions in LK99

LK99 的温度诱导相变研究

First author; RINDSTU 393 independent study, supervised by Prof. Xiawa Wang, Duke Kunshan University

第一作者；RINDSTU 393 独立研究，指导教师：王霞蔚教授，昆山杜克大学

Synthesized Li3Re3Te2O12 and Pb9Cu(PO4)6O samples and characterized them by Raman spectroscopy, XRD, resistivity testing, DSC, and DFT calculations. Presented at MC17.

合成 Li3Re3Te2O12 与 Pb9Cu(PO4)6O 样品，并通过拉曼光谱、XRD、电阻率测试、 DSC 及 DFT 计算进行表征。成果于 MC17 会议展示。

LLM-based Sentiment Analysis in Finance

基于大语言模型的金融情感分析

Advised by Prof. David Ye, Duke University

指导教师：叶大卫教授，杜克大学

Built an end-to-end real-time pipeline for news sentiment analysis. Compared ChatGPT vs FinBERT vs BERT on streaming financial news for stock prediction tasks, with model endpoints deployed on AWS.

构建了端到端实时新闻情感分析流水线，对比 ChatGPT、FinBERT 和 BERT 在流式金融新闻上的股票预测表现，并将模型端点部署于 AWS。

Python package for proportional heatmap visualization and spectral reordering, developed by Juntang Wang. I use it in our joint configuration work and contributed feedback from the BICS 2025 experiments.

用于比例热图可视化与谱重排序的 Python 包，由王俊棠开发。 我在双方合作的 configuration 研究中使用该工具，并基于 BICS 2025 的实验提供了反馈。

sgtsnepi

Pure Python port of the SG-t-SNE-Π algorithm for embedding sparse graphs into 2D/3D space, developed by Juntang Wang. Used for the graph embeddings in our shared research.

SG-t-SNE-Π 算法的纯 Python 实现，用于将稀疏图嵌入二维/三维空间， 由王俊棠开发。用于双方合作研究中的图嵌入部分。
